Create a polished flat illustrated emblem: one chunky rounded battle interface window with a large simple circular creature-ball symbol integrated in its upper left, a clearly visible cyan health bar across its upper right, and four large battle command tiles in a two by two grid below; lower right tile violet, one cyan tile with a simple cream triangular selection cursor. Dark navy background, cream window edge, cyan and violet accents inspired by the mod's existing battle palette. Bold clean silhouette, ample margins, minimal shapes, crisp edges, readable at 48px. Straight-on view. No text, letters, numbers, creatures, official logos, watermark, or fine decorative details. Full square opaque background. Produce one final icon.
