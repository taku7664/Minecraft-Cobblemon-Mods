The distribution bundles Cobblemon UI as a nested JAR. That JAR contains LICENSE_cobblemon_ui, NOTICE.md and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE_CobblemonExtendedBattleUI. Its early retained implementation and popup frame preserve the upstream MIT notice. Its NOTICE also identifies two UI texture files whose original provenance remains unresolved. Bundling does not resolve that outstanding provenance question.

Minecraft, Cobblemon and optional Better Cobblemon Battlecam are external dependencies, not assets licensed by this module. The wand references Minecraft's blaze-rod texture; it does not copy that texture into this JAR. RCT trainer skins are loaded from an enabled external resource pack and are not bundled.

Documentation screenshots show Minecraft and an external RCT trainer skin. The module's MIT license does not claim ownership of third-party artwork or grant redistribution rights to the skin pack. The generated generic icon contains no intentionally depicted franchise character; see docs/MEDIA.md for its origin.
